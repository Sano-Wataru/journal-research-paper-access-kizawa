TITLE: Estimation of Physical Activity Level of People in Office Environment by LiDAR Sensor Network

----------- Overall evaluation -----------
SCORE: 4 (conditional accept)
----- TEXT:
This paper proposes a system for estimating the activity levels of office workers using a LiDAR sensor network. Compared with RGB cameras and wearable devices, the proposed approach has the potential to reduce privacy concerns and eliminate the burden of wearing sensing devices. The implementation of an actual LiDAR-based system and its evaluation with 37 participants are also commendable. Furthermore, the paper investigates the effectiveness of multiple feature representations by comparing image-based and centroid-based features using Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers, which constitutes a valuable contribution.

However, the following issues should be addressed before the paper can be accepted for publication.
Please revise the manuscripts according to all these comments.These are mandatory.

1. Clearly justify the validity of the ground-truth labeling method.
The activity labels are generated by applying k-means clustering to quiz scores and questionnaire responses. However, the paper does not sufficiently justify why these clustered labels appropriately represent activity level or concentration. The relationship between the generated labels and the intended activity level should be clearly explained, together with the rationale for using these labels as the ground truth for supervised learning.

2. More clearly demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
The highest reported accuracy is approximately 59%, and the performance improvement over the individual feature models is relatively limited. The authors should discuss the practical significance of this performance gain compared with baseline methods and provide a clearer explanation of why the proposed method is effective despite the modest improvement.

3. Discuss the applicability and limitations of the proposed method.
The evaluation is conducted only under a limited scenario in which students watch SQL educational videos. The authors should discuss the applicability of the proposed method to real office environments involving more diverse deskwork activities, as well as the limitations of the current evaluation scenario.

Please revise the manuscripts in the following points.

1) It is hard to see how this differs from the author's previous research. If building the estimation model is the key distinction in this work, the novelty of that approach must be clearly highlighted. Since it does not appear that a new model was devised in this study, it is necessary to explain the criteria used to select this model, based on the overall design guidelines for the system.

2) The benefits of distributing processing tasks among edge devices, computers, and servers need to be clarified. Although the term "edge" is used in all three, please explain why you specifically use the term "edge". If any processing is to be performed in the cloud, please specify exactly what will be processed there.
If the challenge of performing everything at the edge lies in computational complexity, the target computational complexity should be specified before discussing the system.

3) Regarding the problem the proposed method aims to solve, it is necessary to demonstrate why it is necessary to construct a point cloud from the LiDAR distance data rather than using the distance data directly. Although multiple-direction LiDAR data are used, it is possible to process them as individual data without integrating them to perform the detection tasks required for this study. The advantages and disadvantages of integration should be clarified. Furthermore, regarding that the point cloud data is projected onto images in three directions, benefits of this approach compared to handling the raw point cloud data should be clearly explained.

4) Regarding English grammer, please double-check your use of tenses in every sentence. Since the proposed method is written in the past tense, it is difficult to grasp what is proposed in this manuscript.
