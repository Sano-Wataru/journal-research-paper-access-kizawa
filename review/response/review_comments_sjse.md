SUBMISSION: 25
TITLE: Estimation of Physical Activity Level of People in Office Environment by LiDAR Sensor Network

----------- Overall evaluation -----------
SCORE: 4 (conditional accept)
----- TEXT:
This paper proposes a system for estimating the activity levels of office workers using a LiDAR sensor network. Compared with RGB cameras and wearable devices, the proposed approach has the potential to reduce privacy concerns and eliminate the burden of wearing sensing devices. The implementation of an actual LiDAR-based system and its evaluation with 37 participants are also commendable. Furthermore, the paper investigates the effectiveness of multiple feature representations by comparing image-based and centroid-based features using Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers, which constitutes a valuable contribution.

However, the following issues should be addressed before the paper can be accepted for publication.
Please revise the manuscripts according to all these comments.These are mandatory.

C.1-1
1. Clearly justify the validity of the ground-truth labeling method.
The activity labels are generated by applying k-means clustering to quiz scores and questionnaire responses. However, the paper does not sufficiently justify why these clustered labels appropriately represent activity level or concentration. The relationship between the generated labels and the intended activity level should be clearly explained, together with the rationale for using these labels as the ground truth for supervised learning.
[原因]
activity level は集中度と定義されている。
ラベルはクイズ得点とアンケート結果（既知度・理解度）を クラスタリング したクラスタ番号 GL である。
ground truthは2セッションのラベルの一致・不一致を二値で表現したMLである。

論文中では、集中度とクイズ・アンケートの関係は仮説に留まり、ML を教師あり学習の ground truth とする根拠が書かれていない。

1節「Introduction」4段落目
"We define activity level as the degree of concentration."
"The activity level indicates whether a person is focused on the task or has lost concentration."
"We hypothesize the following. Concentration is accompanied by active movements."
activity level の定義と仮説。クイズ・アンケートが集中度を測る根拠ではない。

1節「Introduction」4段落目
"We obtained several indicators representing the activity levels of people during work using objective evaluation and subjective evaluation of the educational video content."
"We created labels for activity indicators using clustering and generated binary labels on the basis of the consistency of labels among subjects."
指標とクラスタリングでラベルを作ると述べるのみ。

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Overview」
"We measured the activity levels from subjective and objective perspectives."
"The subjective evaluation was based on prior knowledge and comprehension of the content of the learning video, while the objective evaluation was based on quiz scores."
activity levels の測定が既知度・理解度とクイズ得点だと述べている。それらを結び付ける根拠は述べられていない。

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Training dataset」subsubsection「Data processing for evaluation」1段落目
"The activity levels were treated as a measure that integrates the subjective and objective perspectives."
"K-means was used as a clustering method to split samples into k groups."
"The number of clusters was set to k=3."
"A GL cluster was assigned to each point-cloud dataset as an activity-level indicator."
k-means の GL を activity-level indicator としている。k=3 の根拠も、GL が集中度である根拠もない。

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Training dataset」subsubsection「Data processing for evaluation」3段落目
"Agreement and disagreement between the GL of the two datasets in each of the point-cloud datasets pairs are defined as matching labels (ML)."
"ML is represented as a binary label."

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Machine-learning model」
"The ML derived from GL is regarded as the ground truth, and the ML estimated by the model based on the features is regarded as the estimated result."
ML を ground truth とする宣言。妥当性の議論はない。

C.1-2
2. More clearly demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
The highest reported accuracy is approximately 59%, and the performance improvement over the individual feature models is relatively limited. The authors should discuss the practical significance of this performance gain compared with baseline methods and provide a clearer explanation of why the proposed method is effective despite the modest improvement.
[原因]
最高精度は XGBoost の All features で 0.590。個別特徴との差は小さく、RF では全特徴が最良ではない。All featuresの有効性を述べるには不十分であり、ベースラインとの比較が必要と判断された。

5節「Evaluation result」subsection「Evaluation with short-video data」Table「Accuracy using XGBoost (short-video data)」
All 0.590、Image-50th 0.538、Centroid-50th 0.538。
最高約 59%。全特徴と最良の個別特徴の差は 0.052。

5節「Evaluation result」subsubsection「Results of XGBoost evaluation」1段落目
"The model with the highest accuracy was the one using all features."

5節「Evaluation result」subsubsection「Results of XGBoost evaluation」2段落目
"From these results, we confirmed that employing a diverse set of features derived from three-dimensional information is effective for estimating activity levels"
精度の大小比較から effective と結論している。

6節「Conclusion」2段落目
"The estimation results demonstrated that three-dimensional features derived from point-cloud data are effective for activity-level estimation."
"For the XGBoost model, incorporating all features achieved higher accuracy than any single-feature model."

5節「Evaluation result」subsection「Results analysis」subsubsection「Impact of clustering stability on estimation accuracy」2段落目
"However, it was also hypothesized that clustering instability might partially explain why the improvement in estimation accuracy remained limited."
改善が限定的であることは認めている。根拠はクラスタの不安定性に寄せており、ベースライン比較や実用上の意味は書いていない。

C.1-3
3. Discuss the applicability and limitations of the proposed method.
The evaluation is conducted only under a limited scenario in which students watch SQL educational videos. The authors should discuss the applicability of the proposed method to real office environments involving more diverse deskwork activities, as well as the limitations of the current evaluation scenario.
[原因]
対象はオフィスのデスクワークと主張するが、評価は学生による SQL 学習動画の視聴・メモ・クイズに限られる。実オフィスの多様な作業への適用と、現行シナリオの限界が十分に議論されていない。

1節「Introduction」4段落目
"Our study focuses on developing an activity level estimation system for PC-based deskwork."
"The deskwork is one of the tasks commonly assumed in office environments."
"We collected point-cloud data of people during multiple short video viewing"
研究は、オフィス環境におけるデスクワークを対象としていることを主張している。

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Overview」
"Our experimental scenario involved ``taking notes while viewing learning video content and taking a quiz afterward.''"
"We conducted data-acquisition experiments to evaluate the features for assessing activity levels using multiple short learning videos."

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Training dataset」subsubsection「Experiment」
"The participants in the experiment were 37 students from Shibaura Institute of Technology."
"Their ages ranged from 19 to 24."
"Three learning videos about structured query language (SQL) were examined"
被験者は学生。課題は SQL 動画3本に関するクイズ。

4節「Description of evaluation」subsection「Evaluation scenario」
"For our evaluation experiment, we selected a scenario that is considered the most common in office settings and involves relatively few complex movements."
"By flexibly adopting the point-cloud segmentation and feature-extraction methods proposed in these studies, our system can also be extended to tasks beyond deskwork."
拡張可能性の言及はある。

実オフィスでの多様なデスクワークを評価した記述はない。現行シナリオを限定条件とした限界は論じられていない。
