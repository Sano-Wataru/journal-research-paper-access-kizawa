----- TEXT:
This paper proposes a system for estimating the activity levels of office workers using a LiDAR sensor network. Compared with RGB cameras and wearable devices, the proposed approach has the potential to reduce privacy concerns and eliminate the burden of wearing sensing devices. The implementation of an actual LiDAR-based system and its evaluation with 37 participants are also commendable. Furthermore, the paper investigates the effectiveness of multiple feature representations by comparing image-based and centroid-based features using Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers, which constitutes a valuable contribution.

However, the following issues should be addressed before the paper can be accepted for publication.
Please revise the manuscripts according to all these comments.These are mandatory.

C.1-1（原稿反映済み）
1. Clearly justify the validity of the ground-truth labeling method.
The activity labels are generated by applying k-means clustering to quiz scores and questionnaire responses. However, the paper does not sufficiently justify why these clustered labels appropriately represent activity level or concentration. The relationship between the generated labels and the intended activity level should be clearly explained, together with the rationale for using these labels as the ground truth for supervised learning.
[原因]
